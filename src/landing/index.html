Quick Install

How to Install in 4 Steps

Compatible with Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera & Vivaldi.

01

Download & Unzip

Click "Download Extension (.zip)" above. Extract the tabflow_extension.zip to any local folder.

02

Enable Developer Mode

Open chrome://extensions and toggle Developer mode.

03

Load Unpacked

Click "Load unpacked" at top-left and select your unzipped tabflow_extension folder.

04

Pin & Enjoy

Pin Tabflow to toolbar for 1-click access. Turn on Allow in Incognito for private tabs.
